Information We Collect

Game Usage Data

When you play our games, we may collect anonymous gameplay data such as:

  • Game progress and achievements
  • Session duration and frequency
  • In-game preferences and settings
  • Device information (model, OS version)

Advertising Data

Our free games may display ads. We or our advertising partners may collect:

  • Ad views and interactions
  • General location data (country-level)
  • Device identifiers for personalized ads

In-App Purchases

If you make purchases within our games, the app store (Google Play Store) processes your payment. We only receive:

  • Purchase confirmation and item details
  • No payment or financial information

Important: We do not collect personal information like your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it through our contact forms.

2

How We Use Your Data

Game Improvement

We analyze gameplay data to improve game mechanics, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.

Analytics

Understanding how players interact with our games helps us create better content and features.

Advertising

We show relevant ads to support our free games. You can opt-out of personalized ads in device settings.

Customer Support

If you contact us, we use your information to respond to your queries and provide support.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to our games
  • We do not share your data without your consent (except as required by law)

Data Protection & Security

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

Encryption of sensitive data
Secure server infrastructure
Access controls and authentication
Regular security audits

Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary:

Data Type Retention Period Purpose
Gameplay Data 2 years Analytics & Improvement
Crash Reports 1 year Bug Fixing
Contact Information Until request deletion Customer Support

Security Note: While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These services help us with:

Google Services

We use Google Play Services for achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves. Please review Google's Privacy Policy.

View Google Privacy Policy

Advertising Networks

We use ad networks like AdMob to display ads in our free games. These networks may collect data for personalized advertising.

Analytics Services

We use analytics tools to understand how players use our games, which helps us improve gameplay experience.

Important Notice

Third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies as we are not responsible for their practices. You can often opt-out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

5

Children's Privacy

COPPA Compliance

Our games are compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Parental Control

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately so we can remove the information.

For Parents & Guardians

  • Our games are designed to be family-friendly
  • We limit data collection for all users, especially children
  • You can contact us to review or delete your child's data
  • We encourage parental supervision of children's gaming activities
